> $$L = \frac{nh}{2\pi}$$

> $$r_n = \frac{n^2h^2}{4\pi ^2 mkZe^2}$$
proof:  
$$ \begin{aligned}
\because L &= rmv \\
\because L &= \frac{nh}{2\pi} \\
rmv &= \frac{nh}{2\pi} \\
v &= \frac{nh}{rm2\pi} \\
\because r &= \frac{kZe^2}{mv^2} \\
\ r &= \frac{kZe^2}{m(\frac{nh}{2\pi rm})^2} \\
\ r &= \frac{kZe^2}{m\frac{n^2h^2}{4\pi^2 r^2m^2}} \\

\end{aligned} $$

> $$E = KE + PE$$

> $$PE = -\frac{k Ze^2}{r}$$

proof:
$$ \begin{aligned}
\because PE &= -\int_\infty ^r F dr' \\
\because F &= -\frac{kZe^2}{r'^2} \\
PE &= - \int_\infty^r -\frac{kZe^2}{r'^2} dr' \\
PE &= kZe^2\int_ \infty^r \frac{1}{r'^2} \\
PE &= kZe^2\left[-\frac{1}{r'}\right]_\infty^r \\
PE &= kZe^2 (-\frac{1}{r}+\frac{1}{\infty}) \\
PE &= -\frac{kZe^2}{r}
\end{aligned} $$

> $$E = -KE$$
proof: 
$$ \begin{aligned}
\because F &= \frac{kZe^2}{r^2}\\
\because a_{cm} &= \frac{v^2}{r} \\
\frac{v^2}{r} m &= \frac{kZe^2}{r^2}\\
mv^2 &= \frac{kZe^2}{r} \\
\because KE &= \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \\
KE &= \frac{kQ_1Q_2}{2r} \\
E &= KE + PE \\
\because PE &= -\frac{kZe^2}{r} \\
E &= \frac{kQ_1Q_2}{2r} - \frac{kZe^2}{r} \\
E &= -\frac{kQ_1Q_2}{2r}  \\
E &= -KE
\end{aligned} $$
