fundemental unit of electricity: Columb ( C )
> $$ e = 1.6 * 10^{-19} C $$
a proton is +e charge, a electron is -e charge

> columb's law: same charge repel opposite charge attract
> $$|\vec{F}| = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q_1Q_2}{r^2}$$ 
$\epsilon_0 = 8.85 * 10^{-12} \; unit = \frac{C^2}{Nm^2}$


> $$|\vec{F}| = k * \frac{Q_1Q_2}{r^2}$$
$$ k = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} $$

Materials and bands: (bands are energy levels)
Insulator: valence band and conduction band are far apart seperated by an energy gap
Conductor: valence band and conduction band overlap
Semi-conductor: valence band and conduction band are close. Voltage gated junction control conductive or insulating.
Super-conductor: resistance is zero, lossless transmission.

to move charge around:
conduction: charge flow through a material
induction: creating electric effects using electric or magnetic fields

Bohr model of the atom:
1. Point like nucleas with positive charge
2. e- orbits are circular
3. e- each orbit is distinct (no in between)
4. e- each orbit radius is quantized
5. as e- move around the nuclues in a particular orbit e- does NOT radiate energy
6. energy orbit n = 1,2,3 have quantized angular momentum
