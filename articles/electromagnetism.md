fundemental unit of electricity: Columb ( C )
> $$ e = 1.6 * 10^{-19} C $$
a proton is +e charge, a electron is -e charge

> columb's law: same charge repel opposite charge attract
> $$|\vec{F}| = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q_1Q_2}{r^2}$$ 
$\epsilon_0 = 8.85 * 10^{-12} \; unit = \frac{C^2}{Nm^2}$


> $$|\vec{F}| = k * \frac{Q_1Q_2}{r^2}$$
$$ k = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} $$
